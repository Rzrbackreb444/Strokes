## The most underserved part of stroke recovery isn't information.  It's someone checking on you.

You leave the hospital with a stack of papers and a follow-up appointment in six weeks. Nobody is watching the days in between. Nobody knows if you did your exercises or skipped them. Nobody knows if you cried on the bathroom floor at 2 AM and decided recovery wasn't worth it.

That gap — between discharge and the next appointment, between what you intend and what you actually do — is where the plateau lives. It's where survivors fall off. Not because they gave up. **Because nobody was there.**

### Not one thing. Everything.

Every tool on this platform is built around one principle: the system should notice when you've gone quiet, bring you back in, and make it as easy as possible to do one more rep. Never Zero.

#### Daily Routine System

5 levels from Survival to Full Power. Morning, midday, evening, and wind-down — every task tracked, every level personalized. It knows when you haven't shown up.

#### Push Notifications That Mean Something

Not spam. One signal per day, triggered by real behavior: a streak about to break, a check-in missed, a Bad Day Protocol activated. No noise. Just signal.

#### Sasquatch Coach — 24/7

An AI coach trained on Nick's voice and philosophy. It notices when you go quiet. It calls you back in. It won't let you disappear without a fight.

#### Survivor Community Wall

Film and share your progress. Introduce yourself. Start discussions. React to wins. The wall is proof — not just inspiration — that people in the same fight are moving forward.

#### 66-Day Challenge with Daily Missions

Not a checklist. A structured program with daily missions across three phases. The system tracks your phase, your completion rate, and your momentum.

#### Recovery Journal

Journal what it took to get through today. Name the wins, name the hard parts. Your story builds day by day — and on the days nothing feels different, the journal proves otherwise.

#### Recovery University

Learn at your own pace. Understand what's happening inside your brain, why the exercises matter, and how neuroplasticity actually works. Knowledge reduces fear.
